**The Crypto That Nobody Uses**

Signal has had a cryptocurrency built in for five years. You probably did not know that.

In April 2021, Signal added a feature called Signal Payments. The currency was MobileCoin. Press called it a breakthrough. Signal had 85 million monthly active users. The integration was the first of its kind.

Almost nobody uses it.

MobileCoin's main blockchain has not received a single code update in 2026. The project rebranded to Sentz in 2023. The founder of Signal resigned in 2022. Jack Dorsey called for Signal to drop the integration last year. The price hit seventy-three dollars in 2021 and now sits at sixteen cents.

The technology was not the problem. Signal kept MobileCoin at arm's length. The feature was opt-in, buried in settings. Signal added a payment, not a stack.

This week, Pavel Durov is shipping the opposite path. The next mandatory TON mainnet update lands Monday. Twelve thousand lines of obsolete consensus code have been deleted. New cryptography lets TON contracts verify Ethereum signatures directly. Telegram is the largest validator on its own network now.

The difference between the two stories is not the technology. It is commitment. When a messenger adopts a cryptocurrency as a feature, the crypto dies. When a messenger adopts a cryptocurrency as its core, the crypto lives.

The five-year results are now visible.
